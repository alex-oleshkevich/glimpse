Bluetooth Details Variant

A Bluetooth popover variant with direct device actions and separate disclosure buttons.

Design Spec

Goal

This variant keeps the simple Bluetooth popover shape, but adds a separate trailing disclosure target for device details and user-facing management actions.

Interaction Notes

  • Main device row area performs connect or disconnect and does not expand the row.
  • Trailing chevron expands details and user-facing actions.
  • Technical fields stay visually secondary and do not appear as first-level actions.
  • Bluetooth Settings remains the destination for advanced management.
