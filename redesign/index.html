Rules

  • Use `redesign/base.css` for shared tokens and components.
  • Use `redesign/components.html` as the component catalog.
  • Put new applet mockups under `redesign/<applet>/`.
  • Keep applets simple: menu rows, headers, separators, sliders, switches, meters.
  • Prefer modern macOS-like control clarity with Xfce-style explicit labels.
